Added a multimodal RAG service: case documents and PDFs are chunked and embedded, and
investigators can ask questions, pull out entities and build a timeline, then export a court
dossier. Answers cite the source chunk so nothing in the dossier is unattributed.

Files never leave the authenticated store — the AI service fetches them through the same
signed-URL path the UI uses.
