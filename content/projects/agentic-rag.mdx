A supervisor agent routes work to Retriever, Researcher, Analyst, Critic and Writer agents on
LangGraph, served by FastAPI and streamed to a React/Vite client over SSE. Retrieval is Qdrant
with bge-m3 embeddings, with input and output guardrails around the graph.

The interesting part is the LLM router: a role-based LiteLLM layer across five free-tier
providers (Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Cerebras, Mistral) with ordered fallback chains, failover
on rate limits and timeouts, and per-model cooldowns — so free quotas stack instead of blocking.
