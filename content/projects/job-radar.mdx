Every morning an n8n schedule pulls remote and part-time roles from Remotive, Arbeitnow,
WeWorkRemotely and LinkedIn, dedupes against Supabase, and has Gemini give each an ATS score
with gap notes. Anything above 50 gets a résumé tailored to the job description — compiled from
LaTeX to PDF by a tiny Express + TeX Live service — and uploaded to a bucket.

A local Vite + React dashboard lists the day's jobs by score, with saved/applied/skipped states,
a "Find jobs now" trigger, and a live activity feed from the pipeline's event log.
