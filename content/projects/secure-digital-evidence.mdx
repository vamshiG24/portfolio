An evidence management platform for law-enforcement and DFIR teams, built as five Express
microservices behind an Nginx reverse proxy and containerised with Docker Compose over MongoDB
and Redis.

Integrity is the product: every upload gets a SHA-256 checksum, and custody is a hash-linked
ledger where each block covers its index, the previous hash, the action, custodian, notes,
timestamp and the file hash — verification recomputes every block, so editing any field in the
database is detected, not just a broken link. Access is RBAC (Admin / Investigator / Analyst)
with mandatory email-OTP 2FA, and files live as authenticated Cloudinary assets reachable only
through short-lived signed URLs.

A Gemini multimodal RAG service reads case documents and PDFs for evidence Q&A, entity and
timeline extraction, and court-dossier export.
